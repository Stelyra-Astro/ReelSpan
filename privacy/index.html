Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Accounts

ReelSpan does not require a user account.

Local app data

Favorites, interface preferences, and other local app settings are stored on the user's device. ReelSpan does not create a remote personal profile for these settings.

Movie metadata and images

Movie metadata and images are retrieved through ReelSpan-operated infrastructure using Cloudflare, Supabase, and The Movie Database (TMDB).

Location

If permission is granted, ReelSpan may use your current location to center the map and prefer films set in your country in the list. The country selection, not a precise GPS coordinate, may be sent with film search requests. Without permission, the United States is the initial list preference. ReelSpan does not intentionally maintain your precise location history. Permission is optional.

Community contributions

If you submit a film or suggest a story-time or story-place correction, ReelSpan stores the submitted film title, identifiers (if supplied), suggested times, places, tags, submission and review dates, review status, and any review notes in Supabase. A random device-generated contribution token identifies your submissions; only a cryptographic hash of that token is stored in the database. These records are used for moderation, publication of accepted corrections, abuse prevention, and displaying your contribution history. Suggestions are not automatically published. No account is required; reinstalling the app or clearing device credentials may make your old contribution history inaccessible on that device. For questions or a deletion request, contact us using the email below; we may need enough details to locate the relevant submission. Please do not include sensitive personal information in submissions.

Tips and payments

Optional tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit. ReelSpan does not receive or store payment card information.

Advertising and tracking

ReelSpan does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

Apple, Cloudflare, Supabase, and TMDB may process data as needed to provide their services. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to their services.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to Stelyra-Astro@proton.me.
