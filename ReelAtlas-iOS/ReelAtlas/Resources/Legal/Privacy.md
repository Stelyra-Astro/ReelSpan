# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

## Accounts

ReelSpan does not require a user account.

## Local app data

Favorites, interface preferences, and other local app settings are stored on the user's device. ReelSpan does not create a remote personal profile for these settings.

## Movie metadata and images

Movie metadata and images are retrieved through ReelSpan-operated infrastructure using Cloudflare, Supabase, and The Movie Database (TMDB). These services provide the app's movie content and image delivery; ReelSpan does not sell this information or use it to build an advertising profile.

## Location

If location permission is granted, ReelSpan may use the user's current location to center the map. ReelSpan does not intentionally store precise location history. Location permission is optional, and the app uses a California map center when a current location cannot be obtained.

## Tips and payments

Optional tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit. ReelSpan does not receive or store payment card information. Tips are voluntary and do not unlock features, create an entitlement, or require an account.

## Advertising and tracking

ReelSpan does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

## Third-party services

Apple, Cloudflare, Supabase, and TMDB may process data as needed to provide their services. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to their services.
